# "The Moth"

Name: Calvus "The Moth" Grieg  
Heritage: Tycheros
Background: Academic  
Playbook: Leech (tinker)  
Vice: Stupor


## Apearance

He bears no strange sigils, no inhuman glow, no unnatural features.
If the daemon blood stirs within him, it does so quietly, beneath the skin, where only his obsessions and compulsions betray its influence.
To the world, he is just another scavenger, another desperate alchemist, another man chasing fire.

A sallow complexion, common to those who work in dimly lit workshops or smoky alleys. His skin carries the faint grime of soot and alchemical residue, giving him a perpetual, ghostly tiredness.

His face is gaunt and angular, with hollow cheeks and sharp cheekbones that make him look like he's always on edge or deep in thought.

His messy once black hair now turning silver is always a little damp or tangled, as if he’s recently been caught in the rain or sweating over a boiling mixture. Strands often fall over his face, shadowing his intense, restless gaze.

His dark brown eyes hold a deep, flickering intensity, but no unnatural glow—just the look of a man who stares too long into fire and sees something in it others don’t.

Years of labor and scavenging have left him thin but tough, with calloused hands that bear scars from burns and chemical mishaps rather.

His long coat is singed at the edges, his gloves are patched and stiff with dried chemicals, and his boots are worn from scavenging across the city's ruins and scrapyards.

## Achemical studies

Master Veylan Solmere plucked him from the docks recognising Clavus'es raw achemical talent inherited from his mother.

Though brilliant, Calvus was reckless, obsessed with pushing the boundaries of alchemy beyond traditional ethics.

One night, he embarked on an unsanctioned experiment, attempting to create a "Liquid Ember"—a substance that could burn perpetually without fuel. His theory was sound, but his calculations were flawed.

In a hidden lab beneath the coastal city of Ikaris Bay, Calvus ignited the prototype. Instead of a controlled burn, the compound exploded violently, sending torrents of ghostly blue fire through the alchemical halls. The flames didn’t just consume matter—they bonded to it, lingering in unnatural ways. Metal twisted, glass melted, and several nearby apprentices were permanently scarred.

His mentor, Veylan Solmere, rushed in to contain the disaster. He managed to suppress the alchemical fire—at great personal cost. His hands and lungs were ruined by the fumes, rendering him unable to continue his work. Though Calvus was responsible, Solmere took the blame to protect his apprentice, claiming the experiment had been his own miscalculation.

The guild expelled Solmere in disgrace, stripping him of his rank, home, and resources. To avoid punishment by the alchemical guild, Calvus fled the Dagger Isles before his secret got out.

Wracked with guilt over his mentor’s sacrifice, Calvus fled aboard a smuggler’s ship bound for Doskvol, taking with him only his salvaged tools and a few stolen research notes. He swore he would one day make amends—but his growing addictions, paranoia, and self-destructive tendencies make that promise feel further away than ever.

## Present day

He's lives in an abandoned warehouse in the Coalridge slums. The building is half-burned and condemned, which means no one comes looking unless they’re desperate (or foolish).

The main doors are chained shut, but Calvus has a hidden entrance through a rotting crawlspace beneath the docks, accessible only at low tide.

The roof leaks, making the place perpetually damp, except for the areas where Calvus has rigged makeshift tarp covers and a messy but ingenious alchemical workshop. The air is often thick with smoke, and the flickering glow of flames and ember-coals gives the place an almost hellish ambiance.

A large, firefly emitting ghostly blue light, kept in a delicate glass-and-wire lantern, provides a dim, eerie glow.
